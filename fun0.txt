# PowerPython Capability List (fun0.txt)

This list outlines the active analysis capabilities in this package, grouped by their parity with MATPOWER (MATLAB) and their respective attributions/licensing.

---

## Group 1: MATPOWER Existing (Standard Parity)

These methods are native to the core MATPOWER MATLAB software and have been ported to Python to ensure full parity.

1. Standard AC Newton-Raphson Power Flow (ACPF - NR)
   - Method: Conventional Newton-Raphson in polar coordinates.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/newtonpf.py`, `power_python/solvers/runpf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER (Ray Zimmerman, PSERC Cornell) under 3-clause BSD.

2. AC Power Flow via Gauss-Seidel (ACPF - GS)
   - Method: Jacobi/Seidel iterative voltage propagation.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/gausspf.py`, `power_python/solvers/runpf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER under 3-clause BSD.

3. AC Power Flow via Fast Decoupled (ACPF - FD)
   - Method: Fast Decoupled XB formulation neglecting conductance/susceptance coupling.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/fdpf.py`, `power_python/solvers/runpf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER under 3-clause BSD.

4. DC Linearized Power Flow (DCPF)
   - Method: Linearized active power flow neglecting resistance and voltage deviations.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/rundcpf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER under 3-clause BSD.

5. DC Optimal Power Flow (DC-OPF)
   - Method: Generation cost optimization using linear DC network approximation.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/dcopf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER using CVXPY for optimization under 3-clause BSD.

6. AC Optimal Power Flow (AC-OPF)
   - Method: Non-convex active and reactive cost co-optimization.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/acopf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER using SciPy NLP minimization under 3-clause BSD.

7. Security-Constrained Optimal Power Flow (SC-OPF)
   - Method: DC-OPF with active N-1 contingency flow security constraints.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/sc_opf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Standard security co-optimization under 3-clause BSD.

8. Unit Decommitment & OPF (UOPF)
   - Method: Combined unit-decommitment and optimal dispatch using heuristic search.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/uopf.py`
   - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER's `uopf.m` under 3-clause BSD.

9. N-1 Contingency Analysis
   - Method: Automated screening of single-line outage violations using PTDF/LODF.
   - Files: `power_python/solvers/contingency.py`
   - Attribution/License: Standard security analysis under 3-clause BSD.

10. Continuation Power Flow (CPF)
    - Method: Continuation predictor-corrector search to trace PV curves and find loadability nose points.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/cpf.py`
    - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER under 3-clause BSD.

11. Sensitivity Factor Analysis (PTDF / LODF)
    - Method: Power Transfer Distribution Factors & Line Outage Distribution Factors.
    - Files: `power_python/network/sensitivity.py`
    - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER under 3-clause BSD.

12. Locational Marginal Pricing (LMP) Decomposition
    - Method: Decomposes dual variables of DC-OPF into marginal energy and congestion pricing components.
    - Files: `power_python/utils/lmp_decomp.py`
    - Attribution/License: Standard LMP clearing under 3-clause BSD.

13. Spinning Reserve Dispatch
    - Method: Co-optimized energy and active reserve allocation across bidding generators.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/dcopf.py`
    - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER's reserve model under 3-clause BSD.

14. Power System State Estimation (SE)
    - Method: Grid state monitoring and measurement calibration using Weighted Least Squares (WLS).
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/se.py`
    - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER SE under 3-clause BSD.

15. Smart Market Auction
    - Method: Simulates competitive electricity market auction using piecewise bidding blocks.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/market.py`
    - Attribution/License: Ported from MATPOWER's market solvers under 3-clause BSD.

---

## Group 2: MATPOWER Non-Existing (Advanced Methods Introduced by Us)

These advanced methods are NOT natively available in MATPOWER MATLAB. They have been introduced to make this package a functional superset.

16. Wirtinger-Based Complex Newton-Raphson (CVNR)
    - Method: Solves power flow directly in the complex domain without decomposing voltages.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/complex_nr.py`
    - Attribution/License: Da Costa, V. M., Martins, N., & Pereira, J. L. R. (1997). "Complex NR Method (Wirtinger)." 3-clause BSD with attribution.

17. Unbalanced 3-Phase Complex Newton-Raphson
    - Method: Phase-coordinate (a-b-c) Wirtinger complex solver for unbalanced distribution system modeling.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/complex_nr_3p.py`
    - Attribution/License: Adapted from Da Costa et al. (1997) Wirtinger framework under 3-clause BSD with attribution.

18. Standard Holomorphic Embedding Power Flow (HEPF)
    - Method: Non-iterative analytic continuation solver using Padé Approximants to guarantee finding the physical high-voltage solution.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/hepf.py`
    - Attribution/License: Trias, A. (2012). "The Holomorphic Embedding Method." 3-clause BSD with attribution.

19. Radial BFS Power Flow
    - Method: Backward-Forward Sweep (BFS) solver optimized for radial distribution feeders.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/radial_pf.py`
    - Attribution/License: 3-clause BSD.

20. Three-Phase Unbalanced AC Power Flow
    - Method: Direct phase coordinate solver using Z-bus iterations for distribution system analysis.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/pf_3p.py`, `power_python/network/admittance_3p.py`
    - Attribution/License: 3-clause BSD.

21. Three-Phase Unbalanced AC Optimal Power Flow
    - Method: Non-convex optimization of 3-phase systems with unbalanced phase configurations.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/opf_3p.py`, `power_python/network/admittance_3p.py`
    - Attribution/License: 3-clause BSD.

22. Multi-period DC Optimal Power Flow with Storage (MPOPF)
    - Method: Dynamic scheduling of active generators and batteries over a multi-step time horizon.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/mp_opf.py`
    - Attribution/License: 3-clause BSD.

23. Stochastic DC-OPF
    - Method: Scenario-based optimization of energy dispatch under renewable generation uncertainty.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/stochastic_opf.py`
    - Attribution/License: 3-clause BSD.

24. VAr Planning (Optimal Capacitor Placement)
    - Method: Determines the location and size of shunt capacitors needed to enforce voltage bounds at minimum cost.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/var_planning.py`
    - Attribution/License: 3-clause BSD.

25. SDP-Relaxation OPF (Convex OPF)
    - Method: Global optimal power flow via Semidefinite Programming relaxation of branch flow matrices.
    - Files: `power_python/solvers/sdp_opf.py`
    - Attribution/License: Lavaei, J., & Low, S. H. (2012) / Molzahn, D. K., under 3-clause BSD with attribution.
